# 📊 Customer Revenue Dashboard

A professional **Customer Revenue Dashboard** designed to analyze customer performance, revenue trends, sales contribution, and key business metrics through an interactive and easy-to-understand dashboard.

The dashboard helps business stakeholders quickly identify **high-value customers, revenue trends, top-performing segments, and areas that require attention**, enabling data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

The **Customer Revenue Dashboard** transforms raw customer and sales data into meaningful business insights using interactive visualizations and key performance indicators (KPIs).

The primary goal of this project is to provide a centralized view of customer revenue performance and help answer important business questions such as:

* How much total revenue has been generated?
* How is revenue changing over time?
* Who are the highest-value customers?
* Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?
* Which products/categories generate the highest revenue?
* Are there customers or segments showing declining performance?
* What are the major revenue-driving factors?

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The dashboard was developed to:

* Monitor overall revenue performance.
* Analyze customer contribution to total revenue.
* Identify top-performing customers.
* Track revenue trends over time.
* Compare customer segments and categories.
* Identify potential growth opportunities.
* Support faster and more informed business decisions.

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard provides an overview of important business metrics, including:

| KPI                        | Description                               |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| 💰 **Total Revenue**       | Overall revenue generated                 |
| 👥 **Total Customers**     | Number of unique customers                |
| 🛒 **Total Orders**        | Total number of orders/transactions       |
| 📦 **Average Order Value** | Average revenue generated per order       |
| ⭐ **Top Customer**         | Customer contributing the highest revenue |
| 📈 **Revenue Growth**      | Change in revenue over time               |

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Revenue Overview

Provides a high-level summary of overall business performance through KPI cards and trend visualizations.

### 2. Customer Analysis

Analyzes individual customer performance and identifies high-value customers based on revenue contribution.

### 3. Revenue Trend Analysis

Tracks revenue across different time periods to identify:

* Growth trends
* Declining periods
* Seasonal patterns
* Revenue fluctuations

### 4. Customer Segmentation

Allows users to compare revenue performance across different customer groups or segments.

### 5. Product / Category Performance

Highlights products or categories that contribute significantly to overall revenue.

### 6. Interactive Filters

Users can dynamically filter the dashboard based on available dimensions such as:

* Date
* Customer
* Customer Segment
* Product
* Category
* Region

## 🧮 Example Business Metrics

Some of the analytical measures used in the dashboard include:

### Total Revenue

```text
Total Revenue = SUM(Revenue)
```

### Total Customers

```text
Total Customers = DISTINCTCOUNT(Customer_ID)
```

### Average Order Value

```text
Average Order Value = Total Revenue / Total Orders
```

These metrics provide a consistent foundation for evaluating customer and revenue performance.
